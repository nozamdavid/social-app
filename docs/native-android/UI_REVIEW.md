# Native Android UI compared with Bluesky

November 18, 2026. Both Android apps ran signed in on the same 1080 × 2400 emulator at 420 dpi. The reference was the [official Bluesky 1.136.0 Android release](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/releases/tag/1.136.0), package `xyz.blueskyweb.app`. Caelum was `am.noz.caelum.native`. Both used a dark appearance. The screenshots below are full, unscaled device captures. Measurements in the implementation notes are logical layout units at the default type scale. Source checks use the [upstream revision imported by the fork](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/tree/44a037a63575c800955eeb9e0a1024e7b2e4c063), which is newer than the reference APK.

## Posts and navigation

1. **Open the selected reply at the top of a new thread.** On the identical nested-reply URL, Bluesky put the author, “It certainly is,” and its GIF beneath the header. Caelum put the root and intervening parent first. The selected reply was below both. This gets worse with longer parent chains. On first entry, position the anchor below the header and leave ancestors available above it. Preserve the existing Back behavior: after scrolling the Bluesky Team feed and opening a post in Caelum, its body returned to exactly **x=168, y=803 physical pixels** on Back in this test. [Native thread list](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/thread/PostThreadScreen.kt#L301) · [Bluesky thread positioning](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/44a037a63575c800955eeb9e0a1024e7b2e4c063/src/screens/PostThread/index.tsx#L264)
2. **Give post actions the same positions and gestures across feed and thread.** In the official capture, Reply, Repost, and Like have counts. Save, Share, and More form a trailing group. The native feed distributes controls with `SpaceBetween`, so changing count widths moves the following icons. The native anchor spreads five icon-only buttons across the page and moves More into the author header. The official header can offer Follow there. In a live tap test, official Share offered **Send via chat**, **Share via…**, and **Copy link to post**. Native Share went directly to Android’s chooser. Upstream also uses a long press for system sharing and a repost long press for quoting. [Native feed actions](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/post/PostCard.kt#L379) · [Native thread actions](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/thread/PostThreadScreen.kt#L879) · [Bluesky control grouping](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/44a037a63575c800955eeb9e0a1024e7b2e4c063/src/components/PostControls/index.tsx#L214)
3. **Reduce the space above the first feed post.** Official Bluesky fits the feed avatar, title, creator, likes, More, and Pin in a single toolbar. The native screen repeats the title below its toolbar, adds a separate action row and a Feed/About tab row. The first post starts much farther down and less of the second attachment is visible. The native capture also omits the compose button and bottom tabs visible in the official capture. Caelum’s route whitelist also hides the tabs in threads and other people’s profiles. [Native custom feed header](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/lists/CustomFeedScreen.kt#L282) · [Native navigation visibility](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/CaelumRootApp.kt#L550)
4. **Restore thread controls and conversation continuity.** Official Bluesky exposes thread options in the header, reply permissions alongside the timestamp, and saves in the engagement summary. Caelum’s header has only Back and Post. Source inspection found the legacy thread traversal flattens a depth-limited response, drops unavailable nodes, and does not consume the saved reply-sort preference. Closing the composer after a successful reply does not insert it or reload the displayed thread. The anchor Reply/Quote actions also remain available for restricted posts, and a failed anchor deletion can still navigate Back. These last paths were traced in code; no replies, quotes, or deletes were submitted during review. [Thread traversal](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/thread/PostThreadScreen.kt#L187) · [Anchor deletion](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/thread/PostThreadScreen.kt#L354) · [Publish callback](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/CaelumRootApp.kt#L773)
5. **Restore the thread preview and the quoted post’s content inside feed cards.** Bluesky groups a feed conversation with avatar rails and a path to the full thread. Caelum Home renders a flat sequence and can show a “replying to” caption. Native quote cards clamp text at four lines and omit quoted media. Upstream allows up to 20 lines and renders the quoted embed with its own moderation decision. A media-only quote can become little more than an author name in Caelum. Native long multiline feed posts also lack the original 25-line “Show more” cutoff. [Native Home](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/home/HomeFeedScreen.kt#L495) · [Native quote card](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/post/PostEmbeds.kt#L573) · [Bluesky quote rendering](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/44a037a63575c800955eeb9e0a1024e7b2e4c063/src/components/Post/Embed/index.tsx#L323)

## Other screens

- **Composer.** The official reply view shows the original author and avatar, an open text canvas with “Write your reply,” and a **Reply** submit button. Caelum shows a compact parent card, a bordered “What’s up?” field, and **Post**. Source inspection found a more consequential difference: Cancel or Back can discard edited content without a draft prompt. Explicitly saving a native draft drops attached images, alt text, and quote context. The official composer also offers camera, video and GIF paths. [Native composer](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/composer/ComposerSheet.kt#L218) · [Original dismissal logic](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/44a037a63575c800955eeb9e0a1024e7b2e4c063/src/view/com/composer/Composer.tsx#L882)
- **Profile.** On the same @bsky.app account, the official Posts tab put the pinned introduction first. Caelum began with a repost. Native can label a pin if it happens to be in the loaded feed, but the author-feed request omits `includePins`. The native expanded avatar is 78dp inside 84dp versus the original 90 inside 94, and the profile actions and banner occupy different positions. [Native request](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/core/network/AtprotoApi.kt#L273) · [Profile header](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/profile/ProfileScreen.kt#L1017)
- **Search.** The official capture has an Explore title, its own full-width search field below, and a numbered Trending section. Caelum places its hamburger and field in one toolbar; the captured first sections were Trending Videos, Suggested Accounts and Popular Feeds. The structural difference persists independently of the actual trends supplied by the server. [Native search](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/search/SearchExploreScreen.kt#L625)
- **Notifications and messages (source review).** Native Notifications exposes All and Mentions. The upstream revision defines All, People I follow, Replies, Follows, and Post alerts. In the native inbox a group can appear with one participant’s name and photo, making the audience look like an individual. Original Bluesky uses the group name and a multi-avatar image. The native inbox avatar is 48dp versus the original 52 on phones. Private conversations were excluded from the visual evidence. [Native notifications](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/social/NotificationsScreens.kt#L96) · [Native chat rows](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/social/MessagesScreens.kt#L497) · [Original chat rows](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/44a037a63575c800955eeb9e0a1024e7b2e4c063/src/screens/Messages/components/ChatListItem.tsx#L199)
- **Lightbox (source review).** The original supports drag-to-dismiss and sharing the actual image. The native implementation supports pan, zoom, horizontal paging and Back, but shares a text URL. [Native lightbox](../../android-native/app/src/main/java/am/noz/caelum/ui/post/LightboxOverlay.kt#L91)

## Implementation order

First address draft loss, restricted actions, and the misleading group-chat identity. For a visible parity pass, build a shared post-action row from the original vectors, match the feed gutters, and restore bottom navigation on detail routes. Then fix cold thread positioning, sort/continuation behavior, quote media, and reply insertion. Repeat the six screenshot pairs at the same density and verify cold entry, Back after scrolling, and short/long presses separately.

The live pass covered phone portrait in dark appearance. Tablet, landscape, light theme, large font, TalkBack, and destructive or publishing flows were outside the device pass.
